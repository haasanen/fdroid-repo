ArmorPaint is a free and open-source tool to paint textures on 3D models.
Built on Godot, it runs natively on arm64 Android (Vulkan) and is the
mobile port of the popular desktop texture-painting application.

Import models (OBJ and more), paint PBR texture sets (base color, normal,
roughness, metallic, AO, emission), and export the result for game engines
and 3D pipelines. No account, no cloud — your work stays on the device.
